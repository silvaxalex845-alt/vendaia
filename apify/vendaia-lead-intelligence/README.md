# VendaIA Lead Intelligence

Find local businesses and turn raw business discovery into prioritized sales opportunities.

## MVP

Input:
- business/category + location queries
- maximum leads
- language
- country

Output:
- business name
- website
- phone
- rating
- review count
- source URL
- lead score (0-100)
- opportunity level
- qualification reasons
- recommended action

## Positioning

This Actor is designed to go beyond raw scraping: the goal is to help sales teams decide **which leads to contact first**.

## Roadmap

1. Better contact enrichment
2. Website quality analysis
3. AI-generated lead summaries
4. Personalized outreach messages
5. CRM/webhook integrations
6. Agentic payments and MCP-ready workflows

## Compliance

Use only publicly available information and respect the terms, robots directives, and applicable laws of the sources you access. Do not use this Actor to collect sensitive personal data or to send unsolicited messages automatically.
